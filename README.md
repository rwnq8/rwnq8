## Featured Research: Ultrametric Quantum Computing (UQC)

<div align="center">

[![UQC](https://img.shields.io/badge/UQC-50%2B_Documents-blue?style=for-the-badge)](https://zenodo.org/search?q=Ultrametric+Quantum+Computation)
[![Open Access](https://img.shields.io/badge/Open_Access-All_Publications-green?style=for-the-badge)](https://zenodo.org/search?q=Quni-Gudzinas)

</div>

**The Problem:** Conventional quantum architectures face a thermodynamic dead end — dilution refrigerators at millikelvin temperatures provide ~50 μW of cooling versus ~1 W at 4 Kelvin. That's a **20,000× gap** that fundamentally limits scalability.

**The UQC Solution:** Embed error suppression in the hardware geometry using ultrametric (hierarchical, p-adic) spaces where the strong triangle inequality provides a natural error barrier — potentially eliminating the **1,000:1 physical-to-logical qubit overhead** entirely.

## Impact Highlights

### 🔬 Published the quantitative case that the quantum industry's trajectory is a thermodynamic dead end — and a viable alternative.

Dilution refrigerators at ~15 mK provide ~50 μW of cooling. Commercial cryocoolers at 4 K provide ~1 W — a **20,000× difference**. Published a manufacturable roadmap for 4-Kelvin topological quantum processing using commercially available hardware.

### 🏗️ Designed quantum hardware where fault tolerance is a property of the geometry, not a software protocol.

UQC uses a discrete hierarchical geometry where small errors cannot accumulate — in contrast to architectures requiring ~1,000 physical qubits per logical qubit for software-level error correction.

### 🏙️ Led the product that scores neighborhoods across America.

Product Manager for the **AARP Livability Index**: integrated 50+ data sources across 7 domains. Cited in **20+ academic and policy studies**. Used nationwide for municipal planning and grant allocation.

### ⚖️ Founded an AI nonprofit. Made national news.

Founded **Empowering Change**, an LLM-powered legal navigation platform for self-represented litigants. Featured in national media for AI-driven legal democratization.

### 🏛️ Managed $1.5M in federal R&D contracts with full procurement authority.

Certified Contracting Officer's Representative at U.S. DOT. Led components of a national forecasting model informing federal infrastructure investment. **Clearance-eligible.**

### 📚 649+ publications, patent portfolio, COR certification, and 20 years of sustained output.

All open-access with permanent DOIs on Zenodo and ResearchGate — no paywalls, immediately verifiable.
